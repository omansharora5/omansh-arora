AI Judge.

Juwi

Juwi is an AI-assisted judging platform for hackathons. The project addresses the workload of reviewing a large submission pool and gives judges a way to work through that material.

01 / Project notes

What we built

The platform applies generative AI and LLM-based analysis to hackathon submissions. It was designed around high submission volumes and the need to support a consistent review process.

02 / Project notes

The event

We built Juwi for Changethon at IIT Roorkee’s National Social Summit 2026 and received a third-place award.

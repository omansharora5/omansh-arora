Project

AI Content Detector.

Text classification

This text-classification project investigates signals that distinguish human writing from AI-generated content. It combines statistical analysis with neural approaches to the classification problem.

01 / Project notes

The task

The system analyzes text and assigns a classification based on its learned and statistical signals. The work focuses on natural language processing and the behavior of text classifiers.

02 / Project notes

Evaluation scope

A classifier output is a prediction, not proof of authorship. I am not presenting an accuracy claim or a claim that the system can identify every model or writing style.

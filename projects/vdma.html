AI Realtime CCTV Crime Detector.

VDMA / VMD Shield

VDMA connects camera input, computer vision, and incident review. It helps a reviewer move from a flagged moment to the footage and context needed to decide what to do next.

01 / Project notes

What it does

Camera feeds pass through pose, motion, and object-analysis stages. Possible incidents are collected with supporting clips so a person can review the evidence instead of relying on an isolated alert.

02 / Project notes

The wider system

The project includes a local review interface, an Android companion, and configurable response workflows. It was presented at GrandHack at IPEC and Binary Hack at RKGIT, with improvements between the events.

03 / Project notes

Development

We are continuing to develop VDMA with a startup launch in mind. The source repository is private. Detection flags are provisional and require review.

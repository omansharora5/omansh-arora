HackRelay.

Local hackathon discovery & WhatsApp matching

HackRelay helps people in a society or college find hackathons relevant to them. Nearby matching events reach participants directly on their personal WhatsApp, instead of requiring everyone to keep checking multiple websites.

01 / Project notes

One event list

The platform supports registering a hackathon locally and bringing in listings from other hackathon platforms. A Python backend collects the event requests and updates used to keep the combined list current.

02 / Project notes

Matching and delivery

The system matches people with relevant hackathons in their area and sends the event information to their WhatsApp. It runs locally and can be operated by another society or college on its own system.

03 / Project notes

College integration

A college ERP integration is planned so event discovery and participation can become part of the existing campus workflow. That integration is separate from the local system already built.

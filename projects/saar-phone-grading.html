SAAR.

Four-camera phone grading system

SAAR is a phone inspection station in development. A phone sits inside a controlled enclosure while four fixed cameras capture its surfaces. The goal is to turn those views into an explainable condition assessment.

01 / Project notes

Capture

The planned camera arrangement covers the front, back, and side panels. Controlled positioning and lighting are important for small scratches, dents, paint wear, panel gaps, and marks that are difficult to judge from a single photo.

02 / Project notes

Local analysis

The target architecture runs computer vision and inference locally. It examines visible damage and repair cues, including inconsistent panel gaps or signs of a replaced display or back panel. These are findings to review, not automatic proof of a replacement.

03 / Project notes

Explain the grade

The review workflow shows the proposed grade, supporting images, and the findings behind it. A QC reviewer can mark a finding correct, wrong, or unsure, add a missed issue, and suggest a correction. Human review also supplies labeled examples for later model work.

VDMA / VMD Shield

A computer vision project built to help people review possible incidents from camera footage and decide what to do next.

What we built

From a camera feed
to a reviewable incident.

VDMA combines camera input, pose and motion analysis, and saved evidence clips in one review workflow. Possible incidents are flagged for a person to inspect. The broader project also includes an Android companion and configurable response workflows.

We presented VDMA at both GrandHack at IPEC and Binary Hack at RKGIT, improving the project between events. These pages describe the shared project; the exact event-specific changes will be added with the event records.

IIT Roorkee National Hackathon.

Juwi / AI Judge

We received a third-place award at Changethon during IIT Roorkee's National Social Summit 2026 with Juwi, an AI-assisted hackathon judging platform.

01 / Project notes

What we built

Juwi applies generative AI and LLM-based analysis to help judges work through hackathon submissions. The project was designed for large submission pools and the workload that comes with reviewing them.
